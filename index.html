Advertising operations, run through the Google Ads API.

D32 Ads Ops is the internal tooling we use to manage our own Google Ads campaigns. Every campaign is built, monitored, and reported through the official Google Ads API — no spreadsheets, no guesswork, and no change goes live without review.

What D32 Ads Ops does

Campaign management

App and Search campaigns, budgets, bids, targeting, and ad assets — created and adjusted through the Google Ads API against the accounts we operate.

Reporting

Spend, impressions, clicks, installs, and conversion data pulled directly from Google Ads, so decisions rest on the account's own numbers.

Review-first changes

Every write is proposed and reviewed before it is applied. Budgets and bidding changes go through a check first, and nothing is applied blind.

How it works

  1. Connect with OAuth. D32 Ads Ops connects to Google Ads with the account owner's OAuth authorization. Access covers only the accounts that authorization grants.
  2. Operate through the API. Campaigns, budgets, and reporting all run through the Google Ads API — the same official interface Google provides to advertisers.
  3. Measure what matters. Installs, registrations, and first purchases are tracked as conversion actions, and bidding follows the numbers, not a feeling.

Built for our own accounts

D32 Ads Ops manages the Google Ads accounts we operate ourselves. It does not manage third-party advertisers' accounts, and it does not resell advertising data. If you have questions about the tool or about how your data is handled, see our Privacy Policy or write to contact@d32adsops.com.
