Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

D32 Ads Ops ("we", "us") operates the D32 Ads Ops tooling and this website. This policy explains what data the tool accesses, how it is used, and how you can revoke access. The tool manages the Google Ads accounts we operate ourselves; it is not offered as a service to third-party advertisers.

1. Data we access

When you authorize D32 Ads Ops through Google OAuth, the tool can access the following data from the Google Ads accounts that authorization covers:

  • Account structure: customer IDs, campaign, ad group, ad, and keyword settings.
  • Performance data: impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, and conversion values.
  • Conversion configuration: conversion actions and their settings.
  • Billing status indicators needed to confirm an account can serve (for example, whether billing is approved).

We do not collect personal data about the end users who see or click the ads. We do not collect passwords. If you email us, we receive the contact details you include in that message.

2. How we use it

The data above is used only to operate and report on the Google Ads accounts it comes from: building and adjusting campaigns, checking spend and performance, and producing internal reports. We do not use Google Ads data for advertising to other people, and we do not sell it.

D32 Ads Ops' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored in access-controlled credential storage on the machines that run the tooling, and are never written into documents, chat logs, or shared files. Access to the tooling is limited to the people who operate it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect stored credentials and account data.

4. Retention and deletion

Account and performance data is retained only as long as it is needed to operate and report on the campaigns. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google account's third-party access settings; once revoked, the tool can no longer read or change your accounts, and we delete the stored tokens for that authorization. To request deletion of any other data we hold about you, write to contact@d32adsops.com.

5. Sharing

We do not share Google Ads data with third parties, except as required by law or to comply with a legal process. We do not sell personal information.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website is a static site. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: contact@d32adsops.com.
